Common questions

Can businesses use Nijata to review vendor or SaaS contracts?

Yes. Nijata is built for individuals, teams, and businesses that need a fast first read of a privacy policy, terms of service, or vendor agreement. It flags data-sharing, liability, and renewal clauses worth a closer look, so procurement and compliance teams can triage documents before a full legal review.

Is Nijata a replacement for legal review?

No. Nijata gives an automated, plain-language read to help you understand a document faster — not legal advice. For contracts that carry real risk or obligations, use Nijata as a first pass and have a qualified professional review anything that matters.

Does Nijata upload or store the documents a business scans?

No. Documents, images, and scanned pages are read and analyzed entirely on-device in the browser — including OCR, which also runs on-device — and are never uploaded to a server. Nijata keeps a private, on-device history of past scan summaries — risk level, word count, flagged phrases — so you can revisit them, but never the full document text, and nothing is transmitted anywhere.

Does analyzing a policy by URL send my data anywhere?

The URL itself does, since a browser can't normally load another site's page directly: Nijata sends the address you enter to a public reader service that fetches the page and returns its text. That's the one input method that isn't fully on-device — file uploads, pasted text, and OCR'd images never leave your device. If you'd rather keep everything local, use Upload file or Paste text instead.

Can multiple people on a team share Nijata's scan history?

Not yet. Nijata's dashboard and scan history live in local browser storage on a single device, so they aren't currently shared or synced across a team.

1. Welcome

Nijata is designed to be friendly for teens as well as adults. If you're under 13, please use Nijata together with a parent or guardian. If you're between 13 and 18, make sure a parent or guardian is okay with you using it, especially if your school or family has its own rules about apps like this.

3. Not legal advice

Nijata gives you an automated, plain-language read of a document — it's a study buddy, not a lawyer. It can miss things or get nuance wrong, especially with long or unusually worded documents. Never treat Nijata's output as legal advice, and don't rely on it alone for serious decisions. When something really matters, read the original document or ask a trusted adult or a real lawyer.

4. How the app works

Nijata reads a file, pasted text, or a scanned/photographed page you provide entirely on your own device — in your browser, using on-device processing and OCR. It doesn't upload your document or image to a server, and it doesn't need an account to work. The one exception is the optional "From URL" option, which has to contact an external reader service to fetch the page you point it at. For the full picture of what little data Nijata does keep or send, and when, see our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy
Last updated September 12, 2026

The short version

Nijata is built to be private by default. A document you upload, paste, or photograph is read and analyzed entirely on your own device — including OCR for scanned pages and images, which also runs on-device. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server we run — we simply don't have a way to see it. The one exception is the optional "From URL" option, described below, which by its nature has to reach out to fetch the page you point it at.

What we actually store

Nijata keeps a private scan history on your device so your Dashboard can show every policy you've checked in one place. For each scan, we save the document's name (or the site's domain, for a URL scan), the date, its risk level, word count, and the flagged phrases and short quotes Nijata found — never the full original document. This all lives in your browser's local storage, is never uploaded, and only your device can see it. You can clear it any time from the Dashboard or by clearing your browser's site data for Nijata.

What we don't collect

  • We don't create accounts, so we don't collect names, emails, or passwords through the app itself.
  • We don't upload, transmit, or keep a copy of the full documents, images, or pages you scan — only the short summary described above, and only on your device.
  • We don't use tracking cookies or sell any data — because we don't have any of your data to sell.

Analyzing a link ("From URL")

Typing a web address into the URL tab is the one part of Nijata that isn't fully on-device, because your browser can't normally load another site's page directly to read it. To make this work, the address you enter is sent to r.jina.ai, a public page-reading service, which fetches that page and returns its text back to your browser for analysis — the same way any website works when you visit it, just relayed through that service instead of a browser tab. Only the URL and the resulting page text pass through it; your uploaded files, pasted text, OCR images, and local scan history never do. If you'd rather keep everything strictly on your device, use the "Upload file" or "Paste text" option instead.

Who we share data with

Nobody, for anything you upload, paste, or scan with your camera. Nijata has no server-side database of your documents or history, so there's nothing to hand to third parties, affiliates, advertising partners, or advertisers. We don't share, rent, or sell your information to anyone, and we've never received a law enforcement request for it — because we have no user data to hand over even if asked. The one narrow exception is the URL you choose to type into the "From URL" tab, which is shared only with the reader service described above, and only so it can fetch that one page for you.

How long we keep it

There's no fixed retention period beyond what sits in your own browser: your local scan history stays on your device only until you delete it or clear your browser's site data — it isn't retained anywhere else, indefinitely or otherwise. The reader service used for URL fetches doesn't receive anything from us beyond that one request, and we have no way to retrieve or delete data from it because we never had a copy in the first place.

Loading the app

To load Nijata's fonts and the tools that read PDF, Word, and image files, your browser fetches a few files from Google Fonts, the cdnjs content delivery network, and jsDelivr (for on-device OCR). Like any website, those providers may see standard request information (such as your IP address and browser type) as part of normal delivery, under their own privacy policies. Those connections only fetch app code, fonts, and the OCR engine — never your document, image, or scan history.

Security

Because your documents, images, and history never leave your device, there's no central database of user content for us to lose or for an attacker to breach in the first place. Nijata is delivered to your browser over an encrypted HTTPS connection. If we ever became aware of a security incident affecting the app's own code or delivery, we'd disclose it here and update the date at the top of this page.

Teens & children's privacy

Nijata is designed with younger users in mind, and it's built to need as little personal information as possible — there's no sign-up, no profile, and no document or history ever leaves your device. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you're a parent or guardian and have questions about your child's use of Nijata, contact us below and we're happy to help.

Your rights & choices

Because everything happens on your device, you're always in control, and you have the right to access, review, or delete your local scan history at any time — you can opt out of keeping any history at all by clearing it after each scan, straight from the Dashboard, or by clearing your browser's site data for Nijata. Since Nijata doesn't hold personal data on a server, most GDPR (EU/UK) and CCPA (California) data-subject request mechanics simply don't apply to us — but if you're covered by those laws and still have a question, contact us below and we'll help however we can. Simply closing the tab clears anything else held only in memory for that session.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We'll never make our privacy protections weaker without telling you first.
